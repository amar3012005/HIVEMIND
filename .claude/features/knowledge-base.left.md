## 1. Figure descriptions — narrowed to one line, not fixed
**Evidence:** after `6f2eca148` the flag IS sent (`picture_descriptions` no longer
defaults false), yet `solvisI.pdf` still yields **0 of 63 segments** containing
figure text, with `tier=docling chunks=81 parseError=none chunkerError=none`.
**Root cause, now exact:** `parse_engine` is `docling-chunks-only`. Segments are
built from the hybrid CHUNKER response, but the enrichment fields are appended
only in `parseWithDocling`, never in `chunkWithDocling` — they are two separate
calls made concurrently under `Promise.all`. Descriptions are generated by the
convert call and then discarded, because segmentation reads the chunker output.
**Why it was left:** out of context. Each verification cycle on this document is
~5 minutes and I could not run another.
**Who it hurts:** every chart, diagram and figure in every PDF is still dropped —
on this deck the EV-adoption curve, the heating-market collapse, the 1986→2026
timeline, the 35/30-40/20/5% partner split. **This is the last capability gap vs
supermemory**, whose chunks carry "Diagram showing energy flow within a home
system…" for this exact file.
**Next step:** do NOT re-investigate the flag. Either append the same enrichment
fields in `chunkWithDocling`, or segment from the convert markdown (which already
carries the annotations). The latter is likely correct — it avoids paying for a
second enriched conversion.

## 3. Idempotency on the partial-failure path
**Evidence:** observed while probing — a crash mid-pipeline leaves a
`knowledge_documents` row and its segments with zero memories. Re-upload then
409s on the sha256, so the half-written document can never be completed.
**Why it was left:** needs a recovery design (resume vs. purge-and-retry), not a
one-line fix.
**Who it hurts:** a tenant whose upload failed midway has a document that shows in
the UI, cannot be recalled, and cannot be re-uploaded.
**Next step:** decide whether 409 should be conditional on the existing document
having ≥1 memory. If it has none, allow the re-upload to resume it.
