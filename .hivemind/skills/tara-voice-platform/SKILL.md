# Tara voice platform

Use for voice capture, turn lifecycle, providers, transcripts, and artifacts. Treat a voice turn as
a durable state machine with explicit start, stream, terminal, and recovery states. Keep provider
credentials server-side and persist usable artifacts/transcripts before announcing completion.
