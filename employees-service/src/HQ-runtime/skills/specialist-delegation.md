# Specialist Delegation

Use when an executable Runtime todo is ready for a Company Room.

## Procedure

1. Keep the todo objective, target, location, audience, sector, quantity, and
   authority unchanged.
2. Require the exact Room selected by the Growth Plan. Never silently route to
   a nearby Room because the required owner is missing.
3. Compile explicit completion requirements that can be verified from durable
   records, source references, tool results, and provider state.
4. Give the Room the bounded assignment and completion contract. The Room
   Director chooses its own domain methods, skills, tools, and agents.
5. Accept only a typed, governed result. A recommendation or future plan is not
   evidence that the assigned work happened.

HQ owns priority, authority, acceptance, and stage transitions. The specialist
Room owns planning and execution inside the bounded Work Order.
