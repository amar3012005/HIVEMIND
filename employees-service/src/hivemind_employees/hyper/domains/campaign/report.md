## Canonical Campaign Contract

Return one structured Campaign Contract, not a second prose report. It must contain the selected strategy and alternatives, company grounding, evidence ledger, audience, channel media plan, creative hypotheses, complete executable actions, coordinated timeline, safety controls, launch plan, measurement and monitoring plan, assumptions, risks, and deterministic readiness checks.

## User-Facing Rendering

The user-facing Campaign dashboard renders directly from this bundle. Agent transcript, raw prompts, internal identifiers, serialized context, credentials, and provider secrets are never report content. Partial or missing evidence remains visible and blocks only the dependent claim or action; it must not be disguised as completion.
