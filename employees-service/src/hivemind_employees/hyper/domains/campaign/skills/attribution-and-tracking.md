---
when: Use when a campaign needs conversion events, analytics, pixels, server-side tracking, attribution, or cross-channel measurement.
---
Define the business conversion and the observable platform event separately. Record the source of truth, event owner, identity boundary, consent requirement, attribution window, timezone, deduplication key, and verification procedure. Distinguish platform attribution, site analytics, CRM outcomes, and experimental incrementality.

Load the matching Claude Ads attribution or server-side-tracking method when implementation detail is needed. Missing pixels, events, consent, domains, CRM mappings, or test receipts block performance claims and optimization, but do not block a clearly labeled awareness-only draft.

Never add attributed conversions across channels as unique people. Never treat view-through, click-through, organic engagement, and qualified pipeline as interchangeable outcomes.
