when: Use when reviewing an existing campaign, diagnosing performance, reallocating effort, or deciding what to pause, continue, or test next.

Establish comparable windows, maturity, spend, delivery, audience, creative, destination, tracking, and business outcomes before diagnosing. Separate observed facts, likely causes, confounders, and proposed tests. Score only dimensions supported by current evidence and an approved deterministic profile.

Load the matching Claude Ads audit, monitor, optimize, math, or platform method for the diagnosed gap. Recommend the smallest reversible change with owner, expected signal, review window, guardrail, and rollback condition. A correlation is not a cause, and a platform recommendation is not permission to write.
