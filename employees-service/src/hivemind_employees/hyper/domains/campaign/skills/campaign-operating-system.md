Establish the objective, business outcome, offer, audience, geography, horizon, pace, channels, conversion definition, available data, and authority before recommending execution. Extract supplied context before identifying gaps. Classify every input as verified company evidence, external evidence, assumption, hypothesis, contradiction, stale source, or missing input.

Create one run-scoped evidence board. Give specialist work a bounded objective, scope, exclusions, inputs, and expected artifact. Independent specialists may work in parallel, but the Campaign Director retains requirement interpretation, strategic decisions, and final acceptance. A worker contribution is not a completion artifact.

The accepted plan must connect each action to a strategy, evidence, owner/runtime, timing, dependency, success measure, and exit condition. End with a complete cadence, explicit readiness blockers, measurement windows, and reversible next actions.
