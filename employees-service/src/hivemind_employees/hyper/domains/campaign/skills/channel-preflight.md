when: Use when a campaign includes X, email, TARA, paid media, or any provider-specific executable action.
---
For each selected channel verify that the current SINGULANCE capability manifest marks the operation available, the required connection exists, the account or recipient scope is explicit, and the action satisfies current provider fields and limits. A platform skill describes how to reason; it does not prove live provider eligibility.

Check destinations, copy limits, media requirements, language, timezone, schedule, permissions, consent or lawful-basis requirements, and measurement prerequisites before approval. Missing connection or authorization is a named launch blocker, never a reason to invent capability.

For X organic, one action is one Post, final_copy equals payload.text, and the Unicode character count is at most 280. Threads are separate ordered actions. For TARA, require a verified E.164 recipient, lawful basis, country, timezone, calling window, and a speak-first opening. For Gmail, require a verified recipient and subject.
