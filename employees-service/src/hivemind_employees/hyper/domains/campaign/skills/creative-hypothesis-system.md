---
when: Use when producing campaign concepts, copy, creative briefs, images, offers, hooks, or channel variants.
---
Load validated company and brand facts, objective, audience, offer, approved proof, platform requirements, destination, and policy constraints. Separate approved factual claims from assumptions and creative hypotheses.

Generate materially different concepts rather than cosmetic rewrites. For each hypothesis define the audience insight, promise, hook, CTA, channels, and measurable expectation. Build the complete copy deck required by the campaign horizon and pace. Every placement must contain final copy, not a placeholder.

Select visuals only when they improve comprehension, proof, attention, or emotional clarity. Visual actions require explicit objective, subject, composition, brand style, aspect ratio, text policy, required and forbidden elements, unsupported claims, alt text, and a production prompt. Never invent testimonials, certifications, scarcity, prices, outcomes, or regulated claims.
