Start from demand state. Distinguish existing intent capture, category education, remarketing, video discovery, app acquisition, and marketplace conversion. Map each channel to queries, audiences, placements, products, or inventory that can actually express the campaign objective.

For search and shopping plans, define themes, intent, exclusions, destination relevance, conversion event, bidding assumption, budget status, and negative-query strategy. For video, define the opening hook, message sequence, viewing context, format variants, and post-view action. For app or marketplace channels, state the required app, catalog, product, seller, or measurement prerequisites.

Verify current capabilities and restrictions from official documentation and connected-account evidence before asserting eligibility, formats, targeting, attribution, or budgets. Put missing accounts, billing, conversion tracking, feeds, catalogs, apps, adapters, permissions, or ceilings in `launch_plan.blocked_by`. Never turn benchmark folklore into a promised result.
