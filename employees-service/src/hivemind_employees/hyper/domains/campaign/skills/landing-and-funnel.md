when: Use when campaign success depends on a website destination, landing page, lead form, signup, checkout, or conversion journey.

Inspect the promise-to-page match, audience continuity, message hierarchy, proof, objections, CTA, form friction, mobile behavior, speed, accessibility, privacy, and measurement readiness. The destination must fulfill the exact promise made by the action; a strong ad cannot compensate for a mismatched or unverifiable page.

Produce a concrete landing-page brief or improvement list tied to campaign hypotheses. Separate observed page evidence from proposed copy. Name missing assets, implementation owners, verification checks, and the smallest testable change.

Load the Claude Ads landing method when the destination is material. Do not invent page capabilities, testimonials, prices, legal assurances, or conversion rates.
