---
when: Use when preparing approval, scheduling, publishing, spending, pausing, resuming, or changing a live campaign.
---
Planning defaults to read-only. Draft a launch plan containing scope, prerequisites, exact proposed actions, affected channels, blast radius, ceilings, approval mode, verification steps, rollback or exit steps, and immutable audit intent.

A write is eligible only when the exact capability is enabled, account or recipient scope is known, company and policy evidence is current, the user approves the exact canonical plan, ceilings exist where spend or volume is involved, and the runtime has an idempotency key, audit record, verification window, and reversible action.

Changed remote state, stale permissions, failed policy review, expired schedule, missing tracking, or incomplete creative blocks launch. Prefer the smallest reversible action. Permanent deletion is never part of an automatic campaign plan.
