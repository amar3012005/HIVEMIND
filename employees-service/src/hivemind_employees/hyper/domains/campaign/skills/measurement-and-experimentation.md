when: Use when defining KPIs, attribution, tracking, experiments, monitoring, optimization, or performance reporting.
---
Define the primary business outcome, platform delivery metrics, first-party measurement, attribution limits, baseline window, review cadence, and decision thresholds before launch. Keep platform-attributed conversions, analytics attribution, CRM outcomes, and experimental incrementality separate.

Every hypothesis states the decision it informs, expected signal, measurement window, minimum meaningful effect where known, guardrails, and stop condition. Never claim causation from organic engagement or sum attributed conversions across platforms as unique people.

Monitoring compares compatible windows with the same timezone, currency, metric definitions, attribution treatment, and maturity. Separate expected learning, seasonality, reporting delay, planned changes, and small denominators from unexplained anomalies. Optimization remains a proposal until the launch-safety gate passes again.
