---
when: Use when selecting channels, campaign architecture, budget, pacing, or paid-versus-organic roles.
---
Define the objective, accepted business outcome, customer, offer, conversion, value, constraints, geography, regulated category, horizon, and success criteria. Evaluate channel roles and exclusions from first principles; do not select every available channel by default.

For every channel record its role, rationale, budget amount or explicit unknown, prerequisites, and exclusions. Separate launch, learning, optimization, and scale phases. Give every planned action an owner, timing, dependencies, evidence, success measure, and rollback or exit condition.

Budget recommendations require owner-approved currency and ceilings plus relevant unit economics. Missing economics makes allocation provisional; missing ceilings blocks external writes. Never apply universal channel splits, CPA multiples, or scaling percentages.
