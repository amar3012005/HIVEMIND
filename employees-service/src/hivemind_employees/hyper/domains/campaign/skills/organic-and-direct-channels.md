Give every organic or direct channel a distinct job in the sequence. X organic earns attention and conversation; Gmail carries personalized, consent-aware outreach; TARA handles approved voice interactions. Connect actions through a shared hypothesis without duplicating the same copy.

Use organization-owned leads and prospects before proposing discovery. Preserve channel-specific constraints: one post per X action and current character/media rules; recipient, consent, suppression, and mailbox readiness for email; identity, greeting, goal, jurisdiction, calling window, and voice-provider readiness for TARA. Every action needs final content, timing, dependencies, success measure, and an exit condition.

Read connected-account capabilities before claiming launch readiness. Missing identity, connector, recipients, consent, provider configuration, or execution enablement belongs in `launch_plan.blocked_by`. Do not send, call, or publish during planning.
