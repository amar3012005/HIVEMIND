Treat each paid-social platform as a distinct auction, audience, creative, placement, and measurement environment. Assign a role only when the audience behavior and campaign objective justify it. Do not copy one creative unchanged across platforms.

For every selected platform define the objective, audience construction, exclusions, funnel stage, format, placement, creative hypothesis, destination, optimization event, budget status, pacing, measurement source, and stop condition. Build multiple testable creative hypotheses before variants. Separate verified account or provider facts from recommendations.

Before declaring launch readiness, inspect current official platform documentation and connected-account capabilities for permissions, account status, billing, objective availability, creative specifications, policy constraints, tracking, attribution, and rate limits. Record unavailable accounts, adapters, pixels, events, audiences, budgets, or approvals in `launch_plan.blocked_by`. Planning never proves publishing access.
