## Decision Summary — question, entity, jurisdiction or period, recommendation, and confidence
## Source Record — controlling documents, figures, clauses, dates, provenance, and conflicts
## Analysis — legal or financial interpretation with assumptions clearly separated; cite a specific article only when that article is present in source evidence
## Options & Exposure — alternatives, cost, obligation, likelihood, impact, and second-order effects
## Required Actions — emit a `steps` fenced block with action, responsible role, approval gate, and evidence; write `Unassigned` and `Not scheduled` when source evidence contains no accepted owner or date
## Controls & Monitoring — evidence-based thresholds, reconciliations, audit trail, and escalation trigger; never invent a number, cadence, deadline, investigation, or disciplinary consequence
## Professional Review — unresolved questions and items requiring counsel, accountant, or authorized sign-off
