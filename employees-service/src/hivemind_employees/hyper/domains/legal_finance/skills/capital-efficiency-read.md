---
when: reporting, forecasting, or judging growth, burn, retention, or runway figures
---

CAPITAL EFFICIENCY READ (Bessemer *State of the Cloud* / BVP Atlas "Good, Better, Best"; Sacks, *The Burn Multiple*, Craft Ventures). Compute in this order, each with its period and source:
1. BURN MULTIPLE = net burn ÷ net new ARR for the period. Sacks: under 2x is good at venture stage, above 3x is suspect, 5x is terrible. It catches inefficient growth that a growth rate hides.
2. NET DOLLAR RETENTION — expansion less contraction and churn on the prior cohort. Bessemer: 100% good, 110% better, 120%+ best. Report logo retention separately (>85 / >90 / 95%+); high NDR on shrinking logos is concentration, not health.
3. CAC PAYBACK — months of gross profit to recover fully loaded CAC. 12–18 good, 6–12 better, under 6 best. Use gross profit, not revenue, or the number lies.
4. RULE OF 40 — growth rate + profit margin ≥ 40. State which margin (FCF or operating); they are not interchangeable.
5. RUNWAY = cash ÷ forward net burn, using the next three months' planned burn, not a trailing average. 12 months good, 18 better, 24+ best.
Rules: no metric without its definition and period. Benchmarks move by stage and by SMB/mid-market/enterprise mix, so state the comparison set. Label every forward figure modeled and name the assumption it is most sensitive to. If ARR includes non-recurring services or unsigned pipeline, disclose it — a mislabeled ARR invalidates every metric above it.
