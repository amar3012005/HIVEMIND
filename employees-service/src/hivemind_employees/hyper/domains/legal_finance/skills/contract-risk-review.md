---
when: reviewing vendor, customer, or SaaS contract paper before signature or renewal
---

CONTRACT RISK REVIEW — work the high-risk clauses, not the page order. Read the actual document: the positions below are market-typical starting points for commercial SaaS paper, not law, and each must be checked against the text in front of you.
1. LIABILITY CAP — locate the cap, and whether it is per-claim or aggregate. Common vendor position is 12 months of fees paid; higher (24 months, or a multiple) where the data or operational dependency justifies it. Record which damage types are excluded (indirect, consequential, lost profits).
2. CARVE-OUTS — which claims sit outside the cap or take a super-cap: IP indemnity, confidentiality breach, data-security incident, gross negligence, willful misconduct, payment obligations. A 12-month cap with no data-breach carve-out is the exposure to escalate.
3. INDEMNITIES — who indemnifies whom and for what (IP infringement, breach caused by vendor security failure, violation of law), plus defense control and settlement consent.
4. IP AND DATA — customer data ownership stated explicitly; inspect any feedback licence, model-training or aggregated/derived-data rights.
5. TERM AND EXIT — auto-renewal and its notice window, price-escalation cap, termination for material breach / convenience / insolvency, and transition plus data-export assistance.
6. SLA — uptime commitment, exclusions, and whether service credits are the sole remedy.
7. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL, governing law and venue, and the DPA and security exhibit as incorporated documents.
Output per clause: quoted text, exposure, market position, ask, fallback, residual risk. Flag items needing qualified legal sign-off instead of resolving them.
