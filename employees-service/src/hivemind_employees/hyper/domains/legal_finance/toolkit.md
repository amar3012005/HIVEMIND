LEGAL & FINANCE TOOLKIT POLICY
- Recall governing documents, company policy, approved financial data, prior decisions, and jurisdiction first.
- Inspect connected source documents and spreadsheets read-only unless a write is explicitly requested and approved.
- Use current primary legal, regulatory, and official financial sources when external verification is needed.
- Contract edits, filings, payments, disclosures, and ledger writes always require explicit approval and professional review where applicable.
