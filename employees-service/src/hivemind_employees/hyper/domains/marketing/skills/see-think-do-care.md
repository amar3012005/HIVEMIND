---
when: a plan mixes funnel stages, or a stage's performance is about to be judged with another stage's metric
---

SEE-THINK-DO-CARE method (Kaushik, *Occam's Razor*, 2013). Build this stage table before assigning any channel job or budget.
1. CLUSTER BY INTENT, NOT DEMOGRAPHICS. Each stage is an audience defined by behaviour that signals commercial intent. Demographic and psychographic segments are not stages.
   - SEE — the largest qualified addressable audience, zero commercial intent. Qualified matters: relevant, but not shopping.
   - THINK — same audience, weak commercial intent: comparing, researching, scoping.
   - DO — strong commercial intent, at the decision point.
   - CARE — existing repeat customers, earning renewal and advocacy.
2. For each stage state three pillars together, never one alone: CONTENT (the asset and the specific claim it makes), CHANNEL (where that intent is expressed), MEASUREMENT (the KPI plus its baseline source).
3. METRIC-INTENT MATCH is the rule this framework exists to enforce. Do not judge a See asset by a Do metric. See earns amplification and reach-among-qualified; Think earns depth, return visits, and likelihood-to-recommend; Do earns conversion rate and cost per acquisition; Care earns repeat rate and lifetime value. Applying CPA to a See-stage asset kills the correct strategy and is the most common way full-funnel work is cancelled.
4. Separate macro conversion (the purchase) from micro conversions (the intermediate commitments) and report them apart.
5. NAME THE MISSING STAGE. Most plans over-serve Do and have nothing for Think and Care. State which stage is unserved and what it costs; a table with four rows and one real asset is a Do plan wearing a funnel costume.
Never invent reach, baselines, or lifetime value — mark unmeasured stages UNVERIFIED.
