## Assignment And Scope

Return the bounded outcome, geography, sector or audience, any explicitly requested quantity, and authority mode. Never invent a quota.

## Qualified Prospects

Return each source-backed prospect with its durable lead identifier, source URL or provider identifier, distinct fit rationale, and personalized outreach angle. Keep facts separate from inference.

## Prepared Actions

Show each prepared email, call, or other outreach action with its linked lead identifier, readiness state, authority requirement, and next safe transition.

## Completion Evidence

Return persistence results, tools and sources used, measurable completion counts, acceptance checks, prohibited external-action count, blockers, and the recommended next action.

## HQ Work-Order Result

In HQ Work-Order mode, return a compact operating report plus the structured result packet. State actual counts and precisely which acceptance criteria passed or failed. Do not label discovery complete unless the qualified records were persisted in the shared lead book. Do not label contact complete unless provider receipts exist.
