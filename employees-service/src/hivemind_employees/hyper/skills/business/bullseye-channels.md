when: choosing marketing or distribution channels, or a channel_mix must be produced with organic and paid lanes
---

BULLSEYE method (Weinberg & Mares, *Traction*):
Most companies default to the channel they already know and lose to whoever found an underpriced one. Work all three rings.
1. OUTER RING — brainstorm every channel honestly: SEO/content, paid search, paid social, offline ads, trade shows, speaking, community building, email, viral/referral, PR, unconventional PR, sales/outbound, affiliates, existing platforms, business development, engineering-as-marketing.
2. MIDDLE RING — pick the 3 most PROMISING for THIS wedge, judged by where that exact segment already gathers, not by channel popularity. Regulated enterprise buyers cluster in analyst reports, trade bodies, and peer references — not in broad paid social.
3. INNER RING — the one channel to concentrate on, with a cheap test that could disprove it, its cost per qualified conversation, and the window to judge.
Output BOTH lanes explicitly: `organic` and `paid`, each non-empty, each entry with a one-line rationale naming why this wedge is reachable there. At least two paid entries. Underpriced attention beats a bigger budget — say what makes each pick underpriced now.
