when: framing an offer, pricing narrative, or why the buyer should act now instead of next year
---

VALUE EQUATION method (Hormozi, *$100M Offers*; Osterwalder, *Value Proposition Design*):
Perceived value = (Dream outcome x Perceived likelihood of achievement) / (Time delay x Effort and sacrifice).
Most offers push the numerator and ignore the denominator, where the real objection lives.
1. DREAM OUTCOME in the buyer's words, tied to the trigger's consequence (the fine, the failed audit, the missed quarter).
2. LIKELIHOOD — the proof that de-risks it: references in their segment, guarantees, pilots, audit artifacts. This is the anxiety-killer.
3. TIME DELAY — how fast the first proof lands. Compress to a first value moment measured in days.
4. EFFORT — what you remove from their plate. Every integration they must build is a reason to defer.
5. Then price against the COST OF INACTION from the trigger, never against a rival's list price.
Name what is included, what is deliberately excluded, and the one guarantee you will stand behind.
