when: defining measures, KPIs, or how a strategy will be proven or killed

ONE-METRIC method (Croll & Yoskovitz, *Lean Analytics*; Ellis & Brown, *Hacking Growth*):
1. NORTH STAR — the one metric capturing value delivered to the customer, not activity. Signups is activity; segment-qualified accounts reaching first proof is value.
2. ONE METRIC THAT MATTERS for THIS stage only. A strategy tracking eight metrics is tracking none.
3. For each: current baseline with its source (UNVERIFIED if none exists — never invent one), the target, and the observation window.
4. GUARDRAIL metrics that must not degrade while you push the OMTM.
5. KILL CRITERIA — the result that would make you abandon this wedge. A strategy with no kill criterion cannot be wrong, so it cannot be learned from.
Vanity metrics to reject: impressions, raw traffic, cumulative totals that can only rise.
