BEACHHEAD method (Moore, *Crossing the Chasm*):
Early wins come from visionaries buying a project; the mainstream buys a proven whole product from references it recognises. Between them is the chasm — crossed by attacking ONE niche narrow enough to dominate.
1. Candidate segments: for each state the compelling reason to buy, the buying committee, and whether word travels between members (a real segment gossips; a demographic does not).
2. Score | Segment | Trigger urgency | Reachability | Reference value | Whole-product gap | Evidence | — cite the evidence lane per row, UNVERIFIED where guessed.
3. Pick ONE beachhead. State the disqualifiers — who you say no to this quarter.
4. WHOLE PRODUCT — everything the beachhead needs to get its problem solved, not just your software. Name the gaps and who fills them (you, a partner, an integrator).
5. Name the adjacent segment the beachhead's references unlock next. Dominate, then expand — never run two beachheads.
