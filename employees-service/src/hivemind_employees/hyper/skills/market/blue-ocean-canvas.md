STRATEGY CANVAS + ERRC method (Kim & Mauborgne, *Blue Ocean Strategy*):
1. Factors the industry competes on (price, breadth, onboarding speed, compliance depth...). 5-9 factors.
2. Plot each NAMED rival high/medium/low per factor, plus the buyer's current non-consumption. Use live findings; cite. If a rival cannot be named, name the incumbent BEHAVIOUR you displace.
3. ERRC grid — the differentiation engine:
   - ELIMINATE: factors the industry assumes but buyers do not value.
   - REDUCE: factors over-served above what buyers need.
   - RAISE: factors under-served that the trigger makes urgent.
   - CREATE: factors nobody offers yet.
4. Value innovation = differentiation AND lower cost together. If your curve merely sits above a rival's everywhere, you have no strategy — you have a bigger budget.
5. Output the ONE claim a rival cannot copy without abandoning their own model. That claim is the differentiator; state where you LOSE too, honestly.
