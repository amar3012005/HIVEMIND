ICP SHARPENING method:
1. recall who the company actually sells to (canon + any CRM/connector data).
2. Segment: for each candidate segment state pain, budget trigger, buying committee.
3. Score segments in a table | Segment | Pain severity | Reachability | Deal size | Evidence | — cite the evidence lane per row; UNVERIFIED where guessed.
4. Recommend ONE primary segment + the disqualifiers (who we say no to).
