when: finding the buying trigger, the wedge, or why deals stall with no objection

JOBS-TO-BE-DONE / FORCES method (Christensen):
Buyers do not have problems, they have a job to make progress on. Find the job, then the four forces around the switch:
- PUSH of the situation: what got bad enough. Prefer a dated external event — regulation deadline, audit, failed migration, new funding, a departure.
- PULL of the new solution: the outcome imagined.
- ANXIETY of switching: what could go wrong, who gets blamed.
- HABIT of the present: what makes doing nothing comfortable.
Push and pull sell; anxiety and habit are why deals die silently. Most strategies over-invest in pull and never address anxiety — name the anxiety and the proof that defuses it.
The WEDGE is the segment where push is dated and anxiety is answerable. Write it as one sentence a founder would recognise as true, naming the segment and the trigger.
