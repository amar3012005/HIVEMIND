STRATEGY KERNEL method (Rumelt, *Good Strategy Bad Strategy*):
1. DIAGNOSIS — name the one structural obstacle. Not "we need more leads"; the reason leads do not convert today. One sentence a rival would find uncomfortable.
2. GUIDING POLICY — the approach that attacks that obstacle and rules other approaches OUT. A policy that forbids nothing is not a policy.
3. COHERENT ACTIONS — actions that reinforce each other. Name what you are NOT doing.
Bad-strategy tells to self-check and remove: fluff (abstractions with no referent), goals mistaken for strategy ("grow 3x"), failure to face the obstacle, dog's-dinner objectives that all rank first.
Concentrate resources: one obstacle, one policy, one segment. Diluted effort across three segments loses to focused effort on one.
